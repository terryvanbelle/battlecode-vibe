## 1. Ideas we never figured out

Notation: **[JWU]** Just Woke Up, **[CF]** confused, **[ON]** Om Nom, **[SP]** SPAARK, **[KR]** The Kragle.
"Ours" cites our repo. Anything marked (inferred) was not read directly.

### 1.1 Macro strategy and win condition

- **Tower flickering / "nuking" / "farms": disintegrate a money tower and rebuild it to convert 1,000 chips into 500 paint.** [CF][ON][KR][JWU][SP] — the single biggest idea we missed, and five of five teams had it (Gone Whalin won the "Most Innovative Breakthrough" prize for it). The mechanism: every tower spawns with 500 paint, a money tower makes 20 chips/turn and costs 1,000 chips, so a money tower destroyed and rebuilt every 50 turns is an effective **10 paint/turn, twice a level-1 paint tower** (Kragle §2.1.2, with the production graph). Concrete rules:
  - [CF] `Tower.flicker()`: money tower, `chips >= 3000`, `paint < 100`, pattern around it intact (`numIncorrectPaint < 3` or chips > 5000), no enemy paint in the 5x5, no enemy mopper/splasher in vision; it messages the nearest soldier with ≥5 paint per missing tile (`FLICKER` message), and `disintegrate()`s only once that soldier is adjacent and the pattern is complete. The soldier (`Soldier.flicker()`) drains `paint % 100` from the tower first, then `completeTowerPattern(MONEY)` immediately. One money tower in three (`robot.getID() % 3 == 0`) is a designated flicker tower and SRPs are kept away from it. Post-mortem: "+70% win rate against my quals submission"; flickering activates above 2,500 chips, paint towers only above 3,000.
  - [ON] "farms": every third tower completed (`getNumberTowers() % 3 == 0`) gets `rc.mark` on its four diagonal corners (`TowerBuild.markCorners`); `Tower.initTurn()` disintegrates when `isFarm && patternOk && a soldier/mopper with ≥50 paint within r²8 && money >= 3000 && (paint == 0 || paint < 100 and no nearby unit is under-full)`. Soldiers repair farm patterns (`helpFarms`) and never SRP next to them. Figures 6–7: 13,000 banked chips spent in <60 rounds, 8 → 23 units.
  - [JWU] `Tower.turn()`: money tower, `chips > 10000`, no friendly tower died in 50 rounds, no enemy paint reachable, an ally within r²8, no enemies visible → `disintegrate()`.
  - [SP] `Tower.run()`: money tower disintegrates every 5th round when `chips > 20000` (ID-scaled) and chips rising and tower count not falling; defense towers that have seen no enemy for 30 rounds also disintegrate. Post-mortem "Better disintegrating".
  - [KR] `Tower.doMicro()`: when `paint < 50 && chips > 2 × cost && pattern complete` and a `SUICIDE` message is delivered to an adjacent robot (who rebuilds it), disintegrate. Also: a safe tower with ≥100 leftover paint and chips > 10× cost spawns a unit immediately.
  - **Ours:** `agents/bob/LEARNINGS.md` #64 / `LEARNINGS_ARCHIVE.md` #67: "`disintegrate()` is a pure suicide with no refund" (verified from bytecode — true, but it reads the engine fact without the economics); alice R2: "culling recovers nothing (`disintegrate` throws and returns no paint)". No lineage ever called `disintegrate` on a tower (`grep disintegrate agents/*/src` → 1 alice test package). Meanwhile our own records describe exactly the problem it solves: alice LEARNINGS A "chip mountains of $113k–$205k", darla `DESIGN.md` "Tower paint is the binding resource against v3": tower paint < 200 on **52.8%** of tower turns, median 187, while chips sit idle; carol's absorbing state (production frozen 1,887 rounds). Flickering is the chips→paint exchange we kept saying did not exist ("paint is the binding resource; chips are not", alice A1).

- **Build money towers first and treat chips as the universal resource; paint towers only to keep a floor of ≥1.** [KR][CF][ON][JWU][SP] Kragle built *only* money towers (and skipped `markTowerPattern` to save 25 paint since the type was fixed). confused: `Util.newTowerType`: money tower if `chips <= 2500`, paint otherwise; §4.1.1 "focused on money towers initially, balance after 7+ towers". Om Nom `Globals.getTowerToBuild`: `numPaintTowers == 0 → PAINT; numMoneyTowers >= 15 → PAINT; money < 2000 → MONEY; else PAINT`. JWU `Robot.determineTowerPattern`: `money < max(1,paint) × 2.5` (×1.5 after 6 money towers) and `chips < 4000` → MONEY. SPAARK `Soldier.predictTowerType`: money:paint target 2:1, money while `towers < sqrt(area)/6`, force paint when `paintTowers == 0 && moneyTowers >= 3`.
  **Ours:** darla `towerTypeFor` is a pure function of ruin coordinates (`k % MONEY_MOD`, `MONEY_MOD = 2`) overridden *toward paint* by a local census (`seenPaint*2 < seenMoney`) and by `chips >= CHIP_RESERVE + 400`; darla finding #8 ("a money tower has `paintPerTurn == 0` — two robots and then a dry build site") drove us to paint-heavy mixes ("paint towers 3:1" in `DESIGN.md`). Top teams accept dry money towers because units refill at paint towers / from each other / by flickering, and because a dry money tower still earns 20 chips/turn forever.

- **Know the global tower composition (paint vs money count) without comms.** [ON][JWU][SP][CF] Om Nom §6: track `max(Δchips)` over a 10-turn window → income; income = `(20 + 3·SRPs) × moneyTowers`; the Jinja template `computeIncomeMap(maxSrps=25, maxMoneyTowers=25)` emits a `switch(highestIncome)` whose cases decode `(moneyTowers, srps)`, then `numPaintTowers = getNumberTowers() − numMoneyTowers`; "whenever our estimated number of paint towers hit 0 we would just build another one". confused `Unit.calculateTowers`: `numMoneyTowers = max(Δchips over 5 turns) / 20`. JWU/SP keep `friendlyTowers.paintTowers/moneyTowers` counts fed by comms.
  **Ours:** darla `censusTowers()` counts only towers *this robot has ever seen* (`SEEN_CAP = 64`, `CENSUS_MIN = 3`); `DESIGN.md` Iteration 25: "carol has no comms and `getNumberTowers()` returns a count, not a composition, so no robot can read the global tower mix". The chip-delta trick gives the composition for free.

- **"Taint"/"cuck" every unclaimed ruin you pass with one blob of paint.** [CF][JWU][ON][SP] A soldier cannot paint over enemy paint, so a single enemy tile inside the 5x5 blocks a tower until a mopper/splasher arrives. confused `Soldier.taint()`: for each ruin in vision with no tower and no ally paint around it, paint the nearest EMPTY tile of its 5x5 (Fig. 5). JWU `Unit.senseNearbyCompletableTowerlessRuins`: `if (allyPaint == 0) cuckLocation = ruin`, painted in `stateInvariantActions`. Om Nom `TowerBuild.unmarkedRuin` → `leaveMark`; SPAARK `Soldier.explore()` end: paint one EMPTY tile of any ruin with no ally paint; `MESSING_UP` mode.
  **Ours:** we were only the victim: darla Iteration 44 "DENIED RUINS" measured that one enemy tile makes a ruin impossible and built `banRuin`/`BAN_CAP = 8` to walk away (24.8–43.2% of soldier turns idle on denied ruins). We never did it to them; `grep -i taint agents/*/src` finds nothing.

- **Soldier rush / "harass" toward the mirrored enemy start, gated by symmetry knowledge.** [CF][ON][SP][KR] confused §3.4 (pre-nerf rush; post-nerf, idle units default toward predicted enemy tower locations; `Util.getBaseToVisit`). Om Nom `Soldier.isHarasser()`: on maps ≤40 wide, the first soldiers of the L2 paint tower go to the three mirror images of the home ruin (`goHarrass`), splashers are harassers by default; Kragle §2.2.6: 2 soldiers per tower, capture an uncontested ruin, rush if every nearby ruin is contested ("90% of maps captured a ruin before turn 20"). SPAARK `SOL_EXPLORE_OPP_WEIGHT = 2` prefers known enemy towers over ruins.
  **Ours:** darla splashers `stepToward(siege)` only when an enemy tower is *already in vision*; exploration is `newExploreTarget()` = farthest of 4 uniform random map points; soldiers never go looking for enemy towers.

- **Defense towers as a conditional, scarce purchase at chokepoints near the centre.** [JWU][SP][CF] JWU `determineTowerPattern`: `< 2` defense towers, ruin within r²50 of centre, and a wall in the 5x2 strips on two *opposite* sides (`defenseTowerNorth/South` or `East/West`) → DEFENSE; post-mortem credits this with the finals wins over confused and Om Nom. SPAARK: within sqrt(35) of centre *and* enemy paint/bots detected; disintegrated after 30 enemy-free rounds. confused: a destroyed tower is rebuilt as a defense tower only if enemies are still around (`Util.getTowerType`, top/bottom marks).
  **Ours:** darla Iteration 55 / `darla109`: a geometric centre key routing ~19% of ruins to defense towers → "measured-and-small", 46.0% unchanged; closed as "the last unused engine mechanic". The difference is the condition (walls on two sides, enemy presence, cap of 2), not the unit.

- **Phase-dependent production ratios driven by global counters, with save-up.** [CF][ON][JWU][SP] confused `Tower.getNextToSpawn`: paint towers early = soldiers only (one splasher first on maps ≤30); r100–250 soldiers:splashers:moppers 3:1:2 (splashers only with >6 towers); r250+ 1:4:2; money towers: mopper if `paint < 200`, splasher if `chips > 1500 && paint >= 300`, else soldier; counters reset at the phase boundaries. Om Nom §13: towers pick a unit from a `SpawnPlan` array (`PAINT_INITIAL = S,S,S,S,M`; `PAINT_LATE = Sp,Sp,S,S,M`; `MONEY_LATE_2 = Sp,S,M`), **save up for it**, then build — "produce units in a fixed ratio". SPAARK weights 1.5/0.2/1.2 (soldier/splasher/mopper) with `soldierWeight −= 0.05 × towers`, `splasherWeight += 0.3 × paintTowers`, deficit-based selection, and after r100 only one spawn per 2 turns and `< 4` allies nearby. JWU: 2 soldiers + 1 splasher cycles while `towers ≤ 5 && round < 400`, then S/M/Sp round-robin.
  **Ours:** darla rolls `rng.nextInt(20)` every turn against `SPLASHER_IN_20/MOPPER_IN_20` with `SPLASH_FLOOR = 2000` gating soldiers above splashers; the realized mix is set by which gate binds (carol LEARNINGS "the realized unit mix is set by the ORDERING of affordability gates"), and `darla170` had to invent "one floor-exempt soldier per three splashers". darla's finding #6 ("never withhold production") is the opposite of Om Nom's save-up.

### 1.2 Economy, paint logistics, ruins

- **Refill is a chip-poverty decision, not a paint-level decision; when rich, let units die.** [CF][KR][SP][ON] confused `Soldier.refill()`: refill only if `rc.getChips() < 2000` (and health > 30); otherwise a low soldier keeps working and is replaced. Kragle §2.3.2: "no other team bothered having their bots refill on paint ... only preserve high-value robots, which was just the splashers". SPAARK `Motion.getRetreatPaint()`: returns 0 when `allyRobots.length > 10` (no retreat in a crowd); threshold otherwise `max(paintLost + 30, 25% capacity)`; "don't retreat unless close to the tower" was a large measured win; units top up when passing any tower with paint. Om Nom §3.2 item 1: never go home while an attack/tower/SRP can still be finished (`reallyGoing` flags; `TowerBuild.enoughPaint`).
  **Ours:** darla `walkHomeIfDry` latches below `REFILL_LOW = 50` for every unit type regardless of chips or allies; `DESIGN.md` Iteration 45 `REFILL_LOW = 100` → −6.37 sd; bob `CLOSED.md` #23 "keep units alive by feeding them" −6 at both doses because tower paint is the same pool. Nobody tested gating the trip on the treasury.

- **Opportunistic drain of any adjacent friendly tower, and towers *pushed* to units; leave exact amounts behind.** [CF][ON][KR] confused `drain()`: a soldier ≤150 paint adjacent to any ally tower with ≥10 paint takes it (splasher ≤270, mopper ≤90). Om Nom `Soldier.runTurn`: "opportunistically steal paint from adjacent towers". Kragle §2.3.1: ten money towers floating 700 unspendable paint → make refilling soldiers leave exactly the amount a unit costs. **Ours:** `refillIfPossible()` only below half capacity, and only from a tower we happen to stand next to; nothing about leaving a build quantum behind.

- **Paint-under-yourself is not free: 5 paint to save 1/turn.** [CF][ON][SP] confused §4.1.1: soldiers do not paint under themselves before r150 (`paintLeftover` gated on round/health/tower count). Om Nom `Soldier.postTurn`: paint own tile only if `paint >= 50` and not within 2 of an unbuilt ruin. SPAARK `moveWithPaintMicro` scores a neutral tile as ally only when it can paint it this turn, and only 1 time in 4. **Ours:** darla `runSoldier` paints its own tile first whenever it is EMPTY (`" slf"`), unconditionally.

- **Spawn placement is a lever: toward the enemy/centre, into the least-crowded tile, and connected to the tower by paint.** [JWU][CF][SP][ON] JWU `trySummon`: choose the spawn tile with fewest adjacent allies (−2 if on ally paint, +1 if adjacent to tower, cardinal tie-break), first soldier toward the enemy diagonal, splashers always `enemyDirection`; exploration direction of the unit = direction it was spawned in. SPAARK sorts `spawnLocs` by distance to map centre. confused `tryBuildRobot(type, exploreLocations[4])` (centre), defensive moppers toward the attacker. **Ours:** `DIRS[rng.nextInt(8)]`.

- **SRPs: gate on tower count and idleness, pack them with a tile-exclusion array, and never park a unit for them.** [JWU][CF][SP][ON] JWU `Soldier.determineState`: `BUILDSRP` only when `completableSRP != null && min(getNumberTowers(), knownTowers) >= SRP_TOWER_THRESHOLD` (= `max(4, 6 − min(w,h)/10)`), a paint tower exists, no enemies visible; `MapData.SRPExclusionZoneInt[w][h]` is incremented for a 9x9 around every ruin and around SRPs/towers under construction, decremented when the obstruction goes, so the "can I build here" query is one array read; `srpGridLocs` anchors a mod-4 grid to the nearest map edge. confused: mod-4 centres `(2 mod 4, 2 mod 4)` folded about the map midlines (`Util.getClosestSRPLocation`), only after `≥6 towers && round ≥150`. SPAARK: greedy placement then a 16-location expansion queue (4 optimal + 12 offsets), 64-long bitmask cache of disqualified centres, `SOL_MIN_SRP_ROUND = 50`. Om Nom: any soldier with nothing else to do marks its own tile (`moneyPattern`), pushes the 4 cardinal neighbours at distance 4 onto `srpStack` on completion. **Ours:** `darla121` (453 marks → 14 completions, soldiers wander off) and `darla122` (parked builder: 55/150, "the game is over before the 50-round activation elapses"), closed; alice axis 12 "gate-unimplementable". We never had the `getNumberTowers() ≥ 6` gate, the exclusion array, or "SRP only when idle"; darla's own re-open condition — "an SRP builder that never pre-empts expansion" — is exactly what these teams built.

- **Mark tower type with one 1-paint `rc.mark` instead of a 25-paint `markTowerPattern`, and keep the target colours in a map array.** [JWU][CF][SP][ON][KR] JWU: secondary mark N/NW/NE of the ruin = paint/money/defense (`towerTypeToMarkDirection`); the 5x5 target colours live in `MapData.tileColors` and `checkAndPaintTile` paints any tile the correct colour as a state-invariant action. confused: W/E mark primary=money, secondary=paint, N/S = defense. SPAARK: W=money, E=paint, S=defense (removed once the tower stands so it can later be rebuilt as paint). Om Nom: W=paint, S=money, corners=farm. Kragle: no marks at all (always money). **Ours:** `workOnRuin` calls `rc.markTowerPattern` (25 paint from a 200-paint soldier) and relies on coordinate parity for agreement.

- **Re-mark a ruin to money if the team is short of money towers; let a better-informed soldier override.** [JWU] `buildState`: if the existing mark says MONEY but `determineTowerPattern` now says PAINT, move to the mark tile, `removeMark`, re-mark. Post-mortem §Qualifiers. **Ours:** no override; the type is fixed by coordinates.

### 1.3 Combat micro

- **Soldier kiting of towers: hit, step out of range, step in, hit — two hits per hit taken.** [JWU][CF][ON][SP][KR] JWU `combatState`: in range → `tryAttack` then `tryMoveOutOfRange(tower, actionRadius)`; out of range and action ready → `tryMoveIntoRange` then attack; multiple soldiers enter on even rounds (`rc.getRoundNum() % 2 == 0 || numSoldiers == 0`) so the tower can only hit one per turn. confused `Soldier.attack()`: attack then move to a tile `> 9` from *both* nearest towers; prefer the tower with less health unless the other is ≥400 weaker; back off at health ≤30. SPAARK `attackMicro`: +400 score for in-range tiles when action ready, +400 for out-of-range tiles when on cooldown. Om Nom §12 "back in and out of tower action range". **Ours:** darla `runSoldier` attacks a tower in range then `moveExploring(ruin)` — no retreat; carol CLOSURE_MAP 7.8 "the attack is completely ungated in code"; darla finding #4 observed "~25 hits per kill". `grep -i kite agents/*/TRAINING_LOG.md` = 0.

- **Hard "never step into enemy tower range" in pathing; defence towers as walls.** [CF][JWU][SP] confused `Util.canMove`: refuse tiles within r²9 of a known enemy tower or r²16 of a defense tower; moppers refuse enemy paint (ally paint only when below 20). JWU `tileScore`: −20 in tower range, −10000 in defense range, bugnav treats such tiles as walls. SPAARK `defaultMicro`: penalty = `paintPerChips × moneyCost × turnsToNext × (attack+aoe)/health`, +1000/+2000 if one- or two-shottable. **Ours:** only splashers back off (`" backoff"` when d² ≤ 9); soldiers and moppers path straight through tower range.

- **Splasher valuation: enemy tiles ≫ empty tiles; splashing empty ground is a soldier's job.** [CF][JWU][ON] confused §4.2: "splashers should hold paint until they have a really good opportunity to overwrite enemy paint"; `Splasher.preprocess`: enemy 10, empty 1, ally/wall −2, +10 for enemy tiles inside an unbuilt ruin's 5x5; fire only when the 3x3 sum exceeds 50. JWU: fire at `max ≥ 4` enemy-adjacent tiles, or all visible enemy paint, or near an unclaimed ruin; bytecode guard at 1,500 left. Om Nom `doAttack`: `bestCount >= 4` enemy tiles. **Ours:** `SPLASH_MIN_SCORE = 14` with enemy = 3, empty = 2, so seven empty tiles (14 paint/tile vs a soldier's 5) trigger a splash; carol LEARNINGS defended this ("the real alternative was NO conversion") because darla fields almost no soldiers — which is the upstream problem.

- **Mopper micro that evaluates move-then-act and act-then-move, swings only for ≥2 targets, and prioritises low-paint enemy soldiers.** [JWU][CF][SP][ON] JWU `Mopper.combatState`: cached 9x9 enemy grid, choose the move+swing that hits most robots, swing only if ≥2, else mop the lowest-paint soldier (`getTargetEnemyByPaint` near home, by distance elsewhere); mopping state prefers enemy paint within r²8 of a ruin. confused `computeBestAction(penaltyFunc)`: enumerate 8 moves × {mop succ, 4 swing dirs} with `2×(paint stolen+5)` and `15/enemy` swing scores minus paint penalty. SPAARK: 5 score = 1 paint; mop with enemy on tile = (5+10+5)×5 = 100, +150 next to a ruin; "consider both attack-then-move and move-then-attack ... we had to refactor Mopper and Splasher for it". **Ours:** `runMopper` mops the first adjacent enemy tile and swings a cardinal direction if any enemy is adjacent; alice RETRACTED "`mopSwing` is a 3-tile ground converter — it drains robot paint", and darla finding #2 declared moppers "superseded" and cut them to 10%.

- **Moppers as paint carriers: transfer to allies at 0 paint, "rez".** [JWU][SP][CF][KR] JWU `refillAllies` (keep 30), `REZING` state (20 paint to a dying ally); SPAARK HS: "moppers transfer some paint if they see an ally with 0 paint"; confused `refillOthers()` (soldiers < 40% before r200, keep 40); Kragle moppers transfer above threshold. **Ours:** never; alice RULES notes robot-to-robot transfer "only moppers may", darla: "unimplementable — moppers are structurally absent".

- **Emergency moppers spawned toward attacking soldiers.** [JWU][CF][ON] JWU: if enemy soldiers in range outnumber nearby moppers, `trySummonInDirection(MOPPER, dirTo(enemy))` with a 25-round cooldown; also a `needMoppers` request relayed by messages. confused `spawn()`: mopper toward the closest paint-carrying enemy within r²9 (max 2 moppers near it), splasher/soldier if tower health ≤100. Om Nom `shouldDefend`: mopper toward any enemy soldier/splasher, 30-round cooldown. **Ours:** towers never react to attackers; darla `DESIGN.md` "A tower under attack is alone" closed the routing idea because no *soldier* was in range — the tower itself spawning the defender was not considered.

- **Tower single-target priority: soldiers first (they kill towers), else lowest HP; prefer the one-shottable.** [JWU][SP][ON] JWU prefers any soldier, lowest HP among soldiers; SPAARK lowest HP unless `hp ≤ attackStrength`, then the highest killable; Om Nom splashers > soldiers > rest. **Ours:** lowest HP of any type.

### 1.4 Navigation

- **Paint-aware movement scoring on every step.** [JWU][SP][CF][ON] JWU `tileScore`: −adjacentAllies on ally paint, −adj−1 on empty, −2·adj−2 on enemy, `safeFuzzyMove` with an 8-slot anti-oscillation ring. SPAARK `defaultMicro`: 20/15 for direction, `−5·penalty·turnsToNext` for enemy/neutral paint (×2 moppers), −per adjacent ally, −20 within mopper reach, −1 for revisits; cooldown-aware ("if you won't be able to move next turn multiply the penalty"). confused's Bellman-Ford weights prefer friendly paint; Om Nom BFS tie-break by paint penalty ("saved some soldiers a lot of paint"). **Ours:** darla `stepToward` is direction-only greedy with ID-based left/right and a stuck-triggered wall hug; paint is never consulted when moving. bob `CLOSED.md` #25/#27 measured de-clumping at +5/+6 of 50 three times ("never negative, mechanism confirmed every time") and closed it *at the bar*; carol closed it at stage 0 for splashers.

- **Bug navigation with a real obstacle memory, and the Om Nom "optimal bug" (bug target + bitmask BFS, ~1.5k bytecode).** [JWU][CF][SP][ON][KR] JWU: stack-based wall following with side flip at map edges and `reachableFrom` ray check; confused: camel_case unrolled Bellman-Ford, fall back to bugnav after 3 turns without progress (`Navigator`); SPAARK/Kragle: camel_case / XSquare bugnav; Om Nom §7: advance a bug target treating robots as passable, BFS backwards from it over vision with bitmasks, pick the best BFS-decreasing neighbour by paint penalty — robust to unit clogging, cuts corners. **Ours:** `darla84` added a bounded bug escape only after `mvStuck` fires; carol closed "navigation/pathfinding family" because "units are blocked by robots, not terrain" — Om Nom's design is precisely the one built for robot clogging.

- **Idle timer → go to the far side of the map; explore in the direction you were spawned; zig-zag toward cardinal targets.** [CF][JWU] confused `Globals.noActionCounter > 10` → `flipLocation` across the centre; 9 fixed explore locations with visited flags shared by comms. JWU: `exploreLocation = extendLocToEdge(spawnTower.directionTo(me))`, re-pick when within 8 or inside tower range; zig-zag waypoints. **Ours:** uniform random far target (`newExploreTarget`), re-rolled after 120 turns or 6 stuck turns; alice E "the soldier is CORRECTLY IDLE on 81.0% of turns".

### 1.5 Communication

- **Use the spawn moment: a tower dumps its whole world model into every newborn unit.** [CF][JWU][SP] confused `Tower.initializeRobot` → `Comms.pushInitializeQueue`: every ruin, friendly and enemy tower location it knows, two 16-bit `(category:4 bits, loc:12 bits)` records per 32-bit message, sent over the next turns while the unit is adjacent; towers broadcast their queue every 20 rounds (`rc.getID() % 20 == round`) and relay. JWU `Tower.turn()`: per-ally `messagesSent` cursor over `friendlyTowers`, so each new unit learns every tower; `Comms`: 3-bit type, 6+6-bit coordinates, 2-bit tower type; towers `broadcastMessage` their own location in rounds < 5. SPAARK `POI`: 144-slot tower/ruin table, 12x12 `towerGrid` index (location/5 — "the cool /5 trick"), relay bit, broadcast every 100 rounds, a `StringBuilder` per slot of robot IDs already told. **Ours:** alice Iteration 60 closed messaging at 0.45% of soldier turns because a soldier near a frontier ruin is never near a tower; `darla156` "a soldier is within message range of a tower mostly at the moment it was spawned — exactly when the tower cannot pay" (closed). Both lineages measured the delivery window and treated it as the obstacle; the top teams used that same window to seed each unit with tower locations, which is what makes "go refill at the nearest paint tower" and "rush the enemy tower" possible far from home.

- **Spend the first turns connecting the unit to its spawn tower by paint.** [JWU] `CONNECTING_TO_TOWER` state: paint the tiles between you and the tower so `canSendMessage` holds; "our crucial innovation, which I saw very few teams implementing even at the finals". **Ours:** none.

- **Frontline / mopper-request messages.** [JWU] `FRONTLINE` and `MOPPER_LOC` codes: a soldier that sees enemy paint on a ruin sets `needMoppers`, sends it to any paint tower it can reach; moppers path to it in `exploreState`. Kragle `REQUEST_MOPPER`/`SUICIDE`; Kragle §2.3.2 "communicating battlefronts ... massive improvements". **Ours:** none.

### 1.6 Symmetry and exploration

- **Cheap, complete symmetry elimination from walls and ruins already seen.** [ON][CF][SP] Om Nom §5: map stored as 60 `long`s per feature (wall, ruin, seen) plus x-reversed copies; `checkVertical` is a `switch(x)` over 60 cases comparing `wall[x] & seen_fused` with `wall_reversed[x] & seen_fused` for the 9 visible rows — ~1k bytecode, "guaranteed to crack the symmetry if it was possible from the information seen"; result shared as 3 bits via `Comms`. confused `Util.checkSymmetry`: per-symmetry `FastSet` of already-checked tiles, only tiles whose mirror is also in vision, bounded by `symmetryCheckBytecodeThreshold = 2500`; default assumption rotational → head to centre. SPAARK: 3 symmetry bits in every POI message, `POI.symmetryValid`.
  **Ours:** `darla72/73` did the right test (terrain in vision vs each candidate) but with `senseNearbyMapInfos(-1)` × `canSenseLocation` × `senseMapInfo` per candidate and overran (`ov` up to 8) → void; `darla74` fell back to a fixed 180° guess; bob #32 sized inferred ruins at 0.53/game; carol "BLOCKED". The missing piece was the representation (bitmask rows), not the idea.

- **Default units toward the map centre / predicted enemy towers until symmetry is known.** [CF][ON][SP] confused §3.4.1 "optimal location for determining symmetry while minimising distance to potential enemy towers"; Om Nom `sym > 1 && !seenCenter → pathTo(center)`; SPAARK `exploreRandomlyLoc` with symmetry targets. **Ours:** random.

### 1.7 Bytecode and engineering

- **Code generation / loop unrolling as the default way to write hot paths.** [ON][SP][CF] Om Nom §8: every file is a Jinja2 template (`templates/*.java.jinja2`, 3,716 lines → 204 Java files), with macros for "objects as local variables", `{% for %}` unrolling of the 7x7 splasher scan, `computeIncomeMap`, debug macros, seeded RNG; "inlining made the micro fast enough to not need bytecode checks". SPAARK `MotionCodeGen.java` (5,005 lines) generates the unrolled Splasher (3,820) and Mopper (4,266) scoring; xorshift RNG instead of `java.util.Random` (~50 bytecode/call). confused: 4,640-line generated `BellmanFordNavigator`. **Ours:** one 959-line file, `java.util.Random` per robot, `senseNearbyMapInfos` loops; alice: "Bytecode is not a constraint for this bot" — true because the bot did little per turn; the moment darla tried symmetry inference it overran.

- **Spend bytecode you have left; guard long scans with `Clock.getBytecodesLeft()`.** [JWU][CF][SP] JWU splasher scan breaks at <1,500 left, `closestCompletableSRP` at >10,000 used; confused `Navigator` uses Bellman-Ford only with >6,000 left; SPAARK `Clock.getBytecodesLeft() < 7600` guards. Om Nom §3.3: fixing first-turn overruns (allocating big arrays on spawn) "gave us a substantial win rate". **Ours:** we monitored overruns (`monitorAndYield`) and voided arms that overran, but never degraded gracefully.

- **Object-oriented state machines or goal stacks instead of one if-chain.** [JWU][KR][CF] JWU: `senseNearby → determineState → state() → stateInvariantActions`, Robot/Unit/Tower hierarchy, `returnLoc` memory so a refilled unit resumes its task. Kragle `GoalManager` stack (push REFILL on top of CAPTURE_RUIN, pop back) — "a huge improvement"; §3.3 proposes the goal priority queue with `shouldStart/execute/shouldStop`. confused: ordered method pipeline (`upgrade, flicker, drain, attack, refill, taint, buildTower, buildSRP, move, paintLeftover`) with a per-unit `state` enum. **Ours:** `runSoldier` is a straight-line sequence; a refilled soldier forgets its ruin (`explore = null`).

### 1.8 Tooling, testing and process

- **A/B every version against old versions on every map, then scrimmage the top-20 and watch the losses.** [JWU][SP][CF][ON] JWU: camel_case's `runmatches`-style script over all maps, an auto-scrimmage script that logs into the site and challenges ~20 teams, 57 versions in 25 days; §TLDR "watch replays ... steal what you understand". SPAARK `runmatches.py`: current bot vs `b1..b20` on 37 named maps with Won/Lost/Tied per map; `copybot.py` to fork a package; "running overnight scrims and hoping for a random improvement is actually very productive". confused §6: "extensive replay analysis, borrowing strategies that looked good from other teams"; Om Nom: Modal cloud tester (`test/modal_tester.py`, 73 maps). **Ours:** `tools/gauntlet.sh`, `tools/tournament.sh`, `tools/benchmark.sh` with 75-map censuses, pre-registered gates and McNemar tests — statistically far stricter than any of theirs — but the project rules forbade scrimmaging, replays against benchmark bots (until 2026-09-14), reading rivals' code, and 2025 post-mortems. Every team above names copying observed strategies as its main source of ideas (SPAARK "Copy other teams", Kragle "Learn from others", confused "Special shoutout to ... my primary source of effective strategies").

- **Keep it simple; iterate twice a day; one gimmick each.** [JWU] "there isn't one thing our bot did that I can point to ... a bunch of tiny basic things extremely well". Our lineages spent weeks on the measurement apparatus (alice LEARNINGS section D has ~30 gate/dose rules) and closed most levers on sizing arguments against self-play opponents; carol: "my accept gate was blind for ten iterations".

## 3. Ideas we had that top teams also had

- Splashers out-range paint/money towers and can siege them from d²=10–16 (darla finding #3, `runSplasher` ring; confused Fig. 9 `distanceSquaredTo == 10`, JWU +5 per tower-adjacent tile).
- Prefer killing money towers / the weaker tower (darla finding #5; confused swaps to the tower ≥400 HP weaker; Om Nom "robust to losing paint towers").
- Blacklist ruins you cannot complete (darla `BAN_CAP = 8`, `RUIN_BAN_ROUNDS`; JWU `visitedRuins` 50 rounds; confused `impossibleRuinLocations`; SPAARK visit timeout; Om Nom `last[x][y]`).
- Never attack an enemy-painted pattern tile (darla Iteration 29; everyone skips `isEnemy()` tiles); leave when ≥2 soldiers already work the ruin (SPAARK `SOL_MAX_TOWER_BUILDING_SOLDIERS = 2`, confused `numSoldiersBuilding >= 2`, JWU).
- Soldier-only opening (darla `round < 100 → SOLDIER`; JWU 2 soldiers; Kragle 2 soldiers; Om Nom `S,S,S,S,M`).
- Upgrade paint towers when the treasury allows, never money towers early (darla Iteration 35; JWU 2,500/3,500; Om Nom cost+1,000; SPAARK while `money − cost ≥ 1000`; confused gives money towers a 20,000 "cost").
- Towers always fire AoE plus a single-target shot at the lowest-HP enemy (all).
- Refill hysteresis: latch low, release high (darla `REFILL_LOW`/half-capacity; JWU 25% → 75%).
- Don't gate paint-costly actions on chips (alice finding #7; Om Nom "never money-bound").
- Deterministic engine → fixed map set A/B harness, versioned bot snapshots, paired comparison per map (our `tools/` vs `runmatches.py`); ours adds pre-registration, 150-game censuses and noise-floor calibration that none of theirs had.
- Finish the pattern nearest the ruin / in a fixed spiral so two soldiers complete it fast (darla `workOnRuin` + marked tiles; confused `rotateAroundTower` "sides first, then corners"; JWU `ruinFillOrder`).
